# Changelog

All notable changes to the public connector and this repository. Dates are UTC.

## 2026-11-21: first public release

- The connector, its data pipeline and the website published as open source (MIT), with fresh
  history. The same code runs https://law-api.fuzzzycore.com/mcp.
- The rules an assistant must follow are in the instructions every client receives: 112 first when
  someone may be in danger, word-for-word quotes with the version and data dates, search again in the
  law's own words, one line for "no court decision found", the not-legal-advice line in Georgian and
  English.
- Past dates are described as the newest matsne version dated on or before the day.
- Free help: the domestic-violence hotline 116 006, free legal aid for every minor, and a check date
  and source for each contact.
- The website law.fuzzzycore.com, with connect guides and a "send this to your assistant" message in
  Georgian and English.

## Before the public repository

The service went live on 2026-11-14 and grew to 24 laws, earlier wordings back to about 2016 and
8,473 court decisions by 2026-11-15. An independent reliability check on 2026-11-21 found the law text
identical to matsne in 234 of 234 articles; see [reliability](docs/reliability.md).
