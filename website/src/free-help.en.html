• Legal Aid Service of Georgia იურიდიული დახმარების სამსახური

    1485 or 032 292 00 55

    A state service. Consultation on any legal question is free for everyone. Every minor is entitled to free legal aid. A lawyer at the state's expense is for people registered as socially vulnerable and the other cases the law names; the service decides on application.

    Phone consultation on working days, 10:00–18:00. legalaid.ge

    Checked 14 November 2026 on legalaid.ge (the site did not answer on 21 November). The right of minors: Law of Georgia on Legal Aid, Article 4¹, checked 21 November 2026.

  • Violence hotline

    116 006

    Help for victims of domestic violence and violence against women, including a place in a shelter or crisis centre. Shelters and support are free, whether or not you go to the police or a court. Run by the State Care and Assistance Agency for victims of human trafficking (სახელმწიფო ზრუნვისა და ტრეფიკინგის მსხვერპლთა, დაზარალებულთა დახმარების სააგენტო).

    Sources: government decree, law, Article 17. Checked 21 November 2026.

  • Public Defender of Georgia საქართველოს სახალხო დამცველი

    1481, from abroad +995 32 299 58 98

    Complaints that a state body, official or institution violated your rights: ill-treatment, unlawful detention, discrimination, domestic violence, children's rights.

    Hotline around the clock; office on working days, 9:00–18:00.

    Source: ombudsman.ge. Checked 21 November 2026.

  • Georgian Young Lawyers' Association საქართველოს ახალგაზრდა იურისტთა ასოციაცია

    032 299 50 76, legalaid@gyla.ge

    A non-government organisation giving free legal consultations: in person on Thursdays, 10:00–17:00, at its Tbilisi office, and online by appointment.

    Sources: gyla.ge/contact, the association's statement. Checked 21 November 2026.

Numbers and hours can change, so confirm on the service's website before you call. Each service decides who qualifies for free help.
